## Detailed
## Cloud Operations Engineer
## Business Purpose / Background
We are a dynamic and growing cloud operations team, responsible for ensuring the reliability, performance, and scalability of our critical applications and services hosted across Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Microsoft Azure. We leverage best-in-class tools and practices to deliver exceptional service to our internal and external customers. Our culture fosters continuous learning, innovation, and a strong commitment to operational excellence.
We are seeking an individual to join our team as a hybrid Service Level Manager (SLM) and Site Reliability Engineer (SRE). This pivotal role will be responsible for defining, measuring, and improving the service levels of our cloud infrastructure and applications on GCP and Azure, while also actively contributing to the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of these platforms through SRE principles and practices. The ideal candidate will bridge the gap between business expectations and technical realities, driving a culture of proactive problem-solving and continuous improvement.
We count on cloud operations engineer to empower users with a rich feature set, high availability, and stellar performance level to pursue their applications objectives. Provides maintenance and support for IT Solutions in the Cloud Platforms.
## Key Responsibilities: Drive Cloud Platform operations following industry standard best practices like SRE, DevOps, ITIL.
**Service Level Management (SLM):**
- SLA/SLO/SLI Definition & Management:
- Collaborate with business stakeholders, product owners, and engineering teams to define, document, and negotiate appropriate Service Level Agreements (SLAs), Service Level Objectives (SLOs), and Service Level Indicators (SLIs) for cloud services and applications on GCP and Azure.
- Monitor, report, and analyze SLI/SLO performance against defined targets, identifying trends and potential risks.
- Proactively communicate service performance and any deviations from agreed-upon targets to relevant stakeholders.
- Service Reporting & Communication:
- Develop and maintain comprehensive service performance dashboards and reports for various audiences (technical, business, executive).
- Lead and facilitate regular service review meetings with stakeholders to discuss performance, incidents, problems, and service improvement initiatives.
- Act as the primary point of contact for service-related inquiries and escalations, ensuring timely and effective resolution.
- Ensure proper status communication to leadership
- Manage on-call rotations and shift schedules
- Service Improvement:
- Drive continuous service improvement initiatives based on SLI/SLO analysis, incident trends, root cause analyses, and stakeholder feedback.
- Execute advanced data analytics on platform KPIs, SLIs, and SLOs to identify patterns, anomalies, and root causes of service degradation.
- Develop data-driven insights and recommendations to proactively identify areas for improvement in service reliability, performance, and cost efficiency across GCP and Azure.
- Ensure proper improvement plans are in place and tracked, collaborating with engineering and operations teams to translate analytical findings into actionable technical solutions.
- Utilize monitoring and logging tools to extract and analyze relevant operational data.
- Vendor Management (Cloud Providers):
- Monitor and review the SLAs of GCP and Azure, ensuring our internal services align with or exceed these commitments.
- Participate in discussions with cloud vendors (both hyperscaler and operations service provider) regarding service performance, incidents, and future capabilities.
- Oversee daily L1/L2 tickets, ensuring correct triage, prioritization and escalation.
- Monitor backlog and unblock the operation teams.
- Incident command responsibility for major incidents.
- Coordinate across all levels of support for incident resolution.

**Site Reliability Engineering (SRE):**
- Reliability & Performance Engineering:
- Apply SRE principles to design, implement, and operate highly reliable, scalable, and efficient cloud services on both GCP and Azure.
- Develop and implement proactive monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions to ensure early detection of issues and minimize mean time to detection (MTTD).
- Conduct deep-dive analysis of system performance, identifying bottlenecks and areas for optimization.
- Automation & Tooling: Collaborate on the automation for operational tasks, deployments, infrastructure provisioning (Infrastructure as Code), and incident response on GCP and Azure.
- Incident Response & Post-Mortems:
- Act as a key participant in incident response, contributing to rapid diagnosis and resolution of critical service disruptions.
- Lead or participate in blameless post-mortems, identifying root causes, contributing factors, and actionable preventative measures.
- Ensure effective knowledge transfer and documentation of incident learnings.
- "Toil" Reduction: Identify and advocate for the reduction of manual, repetitive, and automatable tasks ("toil") through engineering solutions.
- SRE enabler: Collaborate on the adoption of the SRE best practices as part of the cloud operations transformation
